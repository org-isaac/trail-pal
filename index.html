Trail Pal

Your pocket-sized partner in uphill crime.

Tool 01

Treadmill elevation

Turn your incline session into metres climbed.

Decimals are fine, e.g. 4.5 km.

Estimated elevation gain

—

Enter your incline and distance to see your climb.

Estimate: distance in metres × incline percentage. For example, 10 km at 5% is approximately 500 m.

Tool 02

Stair stepper elevation

Every floor counts. See how high you've climbed.

Whole numbers only. One floor = 3.25 metres.

Estimated elevation gain

—

Enter your floors to see your climb.

Based on 3.25 metres of elevation per floor.
